Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains how this ABDO AUTO LOCATION website handles information when you browse its pages or use the rental inquiry tool.

Information you enter

The inquiry tool uses your selected vehicle category and dates to prepare a message in your browser. It does not send a request to the business, confirm a booking, or store your selections in a database. Entries remain in the current page and may be cleared when you close or reload it.

Copying and sharing

Selecting “Copy inquiry” writes your message to your device’s clipboard, subject to your browser’s permissions. You decide whether and where to share it. Messaging services have their own privacy practices. Information you share directly with the business is handled through that separate communication.

External resources and technical information

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts and images from Unsplash. These providers receive technical information needed to deliver those resources, which can include your IP address, browser details, and referring page, depending on your settings. The hosting provider may also process access logs for delivery, security, and maintenance.

See the Google Privacy Policy and Unsplash Privacy Policy for their practices.

Cookies and storage

This website’s application code does not set cookies, use local storage, or include advertising trackers or analytics. Hosting infrastructure and external resource providers may process technical data under their respective policies.

Retention and your choices

The inquiry tool keeps no server-side record of entries. A copied inquiry can remain on your clipboard until replaced or cleared. You can close the page, clear your clipboard, and manage external requests using browser settings. Hosting and external-provider logs follow their respective retention practices.

Privacy questions

For questions about information you share with ABDO AUTO LOCATION, including requests to access, correct, or delete it, contact the business through the channel used to arrange your rental. No verified business contact details have been supplied for this website.

Policy updates

Review this policy when the website’s features, hosting, contact details, or data practices change. The date above identifies this version.
